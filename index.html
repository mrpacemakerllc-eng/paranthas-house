From our kitchen

What we cook

Our Story

A family kitchen in Orlando

Paranthas House started around our own table. Rachida cooks the Moroccan side of the menu: slow tagines, harira, fresh breads and the cookies she bakes for every gathering. Eric brings the Indian side: parathas, dal and chana, made the way his family makes them.

We keep the menu vegetarian so everyone at the table can eat everything on it. We cook each order the day it's delivered, and we deliver it ourselves.

Whether it's lunch for a clinic team or dinner for fifty, we'd love to cook for you.

Rachida & Eric
How to order

Three steps, 24 hours ahead

  1. Tell us about your eventDate, time, number of guests and address, by text, phone, email or the form below.
  2. Get your quoteWe reply with a menu and price, usually the same day.
  3. We cook and deliverMade fresh that morning and set up at your location.
How much notice do you need?

At least 24 hours for boxed lunches and packages. For events over 50 guests, please give us a week.

Is everything vegetarian?

Yes. Our kitchen never cooks meat, fish or poultry. Many dishes are vegan or gluten-free.

Can you handle allergies?

Tell us about any allergies when you order. We label every dish and can adjust most recipes.

Where do you deliver?

Orlando, Winter Park and nearby areas. Ask us about locations farther out.

How do I pay?

We send an invoice when we confirm your order. Cards are accepted.

Can I change my headcount?

Yes, up to 24 hours before delivery.
